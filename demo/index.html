1. Temporal Locality (“Reuse in Time”)

Data used right now will likely be needed again very soon (like loop variables). Keeping it on our counter hits 99.22% on loop.

2. Spatial Locality (“Neighbors Together”)

On a miss we fetch 4 neighboring words at once (16 Bytes). The next 3 words then hit in 1 cycle each → 75.00% hits on arrays.

Easy Key Rules

  • Fast Hit (1 cyc): Mealy logic in S_IDLE replies in the arrival cycle.
  • Miss Formula: W×(L+1)+2 = 4×5+2 = 22 cycles (penalty = 21).
  • No Half-Filled Lines: tag_we & valid_we activate only on Word 3.
  • No Double-Counting: stat_miss pulses once before leaving S_IDLE.

Why Each Storage Design Choice Matters

  • tag_ram (64×22): Async read, no reset → packs cleanly into LUTRAM.
  • valid_ram (64 FFs): Flat 64-bit vector clears all lines in 1 cycle (prevents false hit at 0x0).
  • data_ram (64×128): Word write mem[i][sel*32 +: 32] updates 1 word per beat safely.
  • FPGA Synthesis: 128 + 22 = 150 RAM64X1S LUTRAMs, 0 BRAM (async read gives 1-cycle hit).
